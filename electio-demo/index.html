How drones fit into the control loop

Drones add visual awareness; continuous telemetry and human approval keep the operating loop grounded.

First-principles operating logic
  • Locate high-cost, low-rate, or hard-to-reach assets where a missed issue has material impact.
  • Use RGB / thermal patrols to flag visible leaks, abnormal heat, flare changes, or damaged equipment.
  • Join each observation to asset identity, SCADA alarms, production history, tank / inventory state, and work history.
  • Rank by expected lost barrels, safety / environmental severity, confidence, and response cost.
Demand-aware action
  • Pair field context with current nominations, transport capacity, inventory, and buyer / price signals.
  • When demand or logistics shift, recompute the preferred production, storage, and dispatch action within operating constraints.
  • Route recommended work to the operator; record approval, action, verified outcome, and realized netback.
Drone + SCADA→Asset event→Rank→Operator decision→Dispatch / repair
Operating boundary: Drones provide periodic or on-demand inspection imagery, not continuous process control. Real-time market response depends on connected production / inventory / nomination data and approved operating actions.
